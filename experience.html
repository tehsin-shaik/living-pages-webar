Living Pages / camera preflight

Ready to bring the page to life?

This browser-based AR experience turns five printed markers into moving 3D scenes. Keep the complete black-and-white border in view while you scan.

View Markers ↓

Camera access is required to scan the printed markers.

Before you start

Use a second screen or a printout

Open one marker full-size on another device, or print the marker pack. The whale scene needs A and B visible together.

Best conditions

Good light, steady hands

Use current Safari on iOS or Chrome on Android for the best chance of compatibility. Camera access requires HTTPS or localhost.

Sound note

Sound starts with you

The hero chapter begins muted. After the camera starts, use the on-screen control if you want to enable its soundtrack.
